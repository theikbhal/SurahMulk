# Onboarding — Surah Mulk

Copy for the four first-launch pages (`Sources/SurahMulk/UI/OnboardingView.swift`).

## Page 1 — Welcome

**Surah Al-Mulk** · ٱلْمُلْك · 30 ayahs · Makkan

> “Indeed there is a surah in the Qur'an of thirty verses which intercedes for a person
> until he is forgiven.” — Sunan al-Tirmidhi 2880

Read it in Uthmani script with English, Urdu and Telugu translations — and keep a daily
chain.

## Page 2 — Your daily habit

A five-minute chain, protected by a reminder.

- **Quick Pass** — the last 3 lines, your daily minimum
- **7:00 PM** — daily reminder, changeable to 4 PM
- **Streak** — calendar view of every day you read

Details:

- Press **Pass** in Quick Pass to mark the day, or finish a full read.
- Streaks shows your current run, best run and month calendar.
- Change the reminder time any time in Streaks or Settings.

## Page 3 — Study tools

Memorise, vocabulary, grammar and translations.

- **Memorise** — one ayah per month + 7/30/90 day revisions
- **Vocabulary** — 333 words · 224 unique · one word per day
- **Translations** — English · اردو · తెలుగు

Details:

- The Grammar tab shows roots, affixes and i‘rāb for every word.
- Fast Read steps through all 333 words at 0.25×–4×.
- Track views assign dates and schedule your revisions.

## Page 4 — Watch & browse

Recitation video and quran.com.

- **Video** — Surah Al-Mulk by Sheikh Sudais (YouTube)
- **Quran.com** — live page quran.com/al-mulk
- **Help** — full Q&A inside the app

Details:

- Everything is offline — only audio, video and the web tab need the internet.
- No account, no analytics: your streaks and plans live on this Mac only.

## Footer of every page
