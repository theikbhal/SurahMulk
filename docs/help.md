# Help — Surah Mulk

In-app copy shown in the **Help** tab (`⌘4` equivalent: sidebar → Help).

## What is the daily minimum?

Open **Quick Pass** and look through the **last three lines**, then press
**“Pass — mark today done”**. That keeps the streak alive. A full read, a finished
**Fast Read** run or a **drill** also counts for the day.

## When do I get the reminder?

By default **7:00 PM every day**. Change it to **4:00 PM**, **both**, or **off** in
**Streaks → Daily reminder**, or in **Settings** (gear icon). macOS delivers the
notification even when the app is closed — grant the permission when asked.

## How does the streak work?

One mark per day you read anything. **Streaks** shows the current consecutive run, the
best run and the lifetime “days read”. The calendar colours every day you completed;
today is ringed in orange.

## How is the memorise plan scheduled?

**One ayah per month**, starting with the month you begin — ayah 1 gets your first month,
ayah 30 gets month 30. Mark an ayah **Memorised** and a revision plan is scheduled for
**+7, +30 and +90 days**; tick each revision off when you do it. **Drill** hides the
words so you can reveal them one by one.

## What is the word of the day?

The **Vocabulary** tab assigns one of the **224 unique word forms** to each day, with a
status (**new / learning / known**) and a **+1, +7, +30** revision plan. The **track
view** lists your assigned dates for the last 14 days.

## What do the vocabulary numbers mean?

- **333** total word occurrences in the surah
- **224** unique word forms
- **109** repeated occurrences (333 − 224)

Tap a repeated word chip to see every ayah it appears in.

## What is in the Grammar tab?

Every word with its **root**, attached **prefixes** and **suffixes**, **i‘rāb** (syntax)
and **ṣarf** (form), plus a **root-frequency cloud** over the whole surah. Tap a word
card to expand the analysis; filter to a single ayah with the segmented picker.

## Which translations are included?

- English — Saheeh International
- اردو — Maulana Muhammad Junagarhi
- తెలుగు — Maulana Abder-Rahim

All bundled offline. **Translations → Side by side** compares the three.

## What are Video and Quran.com?

**Video** plays *Surah Al-Mulk full by Sheikh Sudais* (YouTube) — in your browser or in
an in-app player. **Quran.com** is a live in-app browser on
<https://quran.com/al-mulk>.

## Does the app track me?

No. Readings, streaks, plans and settings live in local files on your Mac. No account,
no analytics; reading works fully offline (audio, video and the web tab need the
internet).
